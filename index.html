Selected Work

A closer look at a few problems I’ve owned: what was at stake, what I did, and what changed.

Agentic AI · Azure Copilot · 2025–2026

Rebuilding Azure Copilot’s chat as an agent customers can trust

The problem

Azure Copilot’s original chat answered questions but forgot context and couldn’t act. Customers wanted an agent that plans and works across their Azure environment, without giving up control over what it touches.

What I did

As product owner for the core chat agent, I wrote the plan for a full rebuild (new APIs, front end, and agent harness) and a staged rollout where each expansion depended on measured quality, with rollback built in. I coordinated 6+ engineering and partner teams on one release plan, took Spaces from a hackathon prototype to a shipped product in seven months, and built a Customer Advisory Board of 10+ enterprise customers.

For responsible AI, I wrote the harms and quality testing plan, covering approval before action, identity and tenant boundaries, prompt injection, and safe retries.

Outcome

The new agentic chat reached general availability for all Azure Copilot users, a six-figure monthly audience, at Ignite 2026, alongside Spaces and custom skills in public preview. Every agent action still runs through the customer’s own Azure identity, permissions, and policies.

Growth · Azure Portal · 2023–2025

Making the Azure Portal work from the first visit

The problem

The Azure Portal is used by millions of Azure customers every month, from first-time developers to seasoned cloud admins. New users needed a faster path to their first success, and AI features needed a consistent, trustworthy entry point.

Modernized onboarding, homepage, and role-based experiences; standardized error-handling UX patterns across the Portal; and orchestrated the Copilot sidecar rollout across six divisions, using A/B tests to refine engagement.

Outcome

34.8K monthly active users on the Copilot sidecar. The AI Foundry Hub reached 60K+ views in week one and cut navigation-to-task time by 30%. Promoted in band in September 2024.

What I’m building · Personal project · 2026

An AI editorial team for my novel series

The problem

Writing Book 2 of a series means tracking plot continuity, character voice, and pacing across hundreds of pages. A single general-purpose chatbot gave generic notes that didn’t know the world or the characters.

What I built

A book-editor agent built on Claude, split into specialized roles: developmental editor, line editor, continuity checker, character-voice keeper, and copywriter. Each has its own instructions, triggers, and knowledge of the series, so the right expert steps in for the question I’m asking.

What I learned

Narrow, well-scoped agents with clear instructions beat one do-everything assistant. Defining when an agent should act is as important as what it does. It’s the same lesson I apply to agents at work.
